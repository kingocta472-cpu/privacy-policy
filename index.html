# Privacy Policy for Driver Ride Picker Driver Ride Picker does not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. ### Information We Collect 1. Phone Number: Used for account creation, login, and credit management. 2. Location Data: Used to define Work Zones (geofencing) and filter rides. Location is processed locally and stored securely. 3. Ride Information: The app uses Android's AccessibilityService to read ride details (like fare, distance, and vehicle type) from ride-hailing apps. This data is only used locally on your device to calculate ride scores and is not transmitted to any external server. 4. Payment Information: Recharges are handled securely by Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details. ### Accessibility Service Usage Our app uses the AccessibilityService API to perform automated clicks and read screen content specifically to identify order details for automation. - We DO NOT collect or share any data accessed through the AccessibilityService API. - This service is used only to provide the core functionality of the app (automated ride picking). ### Data Security Your phone number and credit balance are stored securely using Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud). We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. ### Contact For any questions, contact us at: driverridepicker@gmail.com
